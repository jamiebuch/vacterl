# Sitemap & information architecture

Goal: small, maintainable charity support-group site. One level deep where possible. All pages reachable in ≤2 clicks from Home.

## Proposed pages

1. `/` Home — Welcome, what VACTERL means in one line, Get-Together hero, 3 CTAs (Get support / Join / Donate), next event date.
2. `/what-is-vacterl` What is VACTERL? — letter-by-letter breakdown, prevalence 1 in 10,000–40,000, diagnosis note (≥3 features, not one cause), Solomon 2011 caveat, antenatal diagnosis challenge. Plain-English + `Easy Read` summary. Disclaimer: information not medical advice.
3. `/support-families` Support for families — peer network, parents/carers, meeting other VACTERL children, self-esteem, signposting (TOFS, Family Fund, IPSEA, SOMA, Birmingham Children's Hospital ARM service). Contact / Facebook group entry route.
4. `/get-together` Get-Together & events — annual meetup, Bristol pop-up / information station photos, volunteers photo (with consent), what to expect, FAQs, past-event gallery.
5. `/stories` Stories & films — `short film who we are` + `Get together 2025` embeds (transcripts + captions), TOFS 2024 meeting summary by Kate Tyler, quotes with consent.
6. `/research-partners` Research & partners — collaboration statement from slide 8, TOFS / adult TOF working group, Family Fund, clinicians (Gail Fitzpatrick, Aled Griffiths as named in article — confirm permission to name).
7. `/get-involved` Get involved — membership form, volunteer / committee, fundraising, `DONATE HERE` (confirm provider + Gift Aid).
8. `/contact` Contact — Facebook group `facebook.com/groups/161861912196/`, Instagram `instagram.com/vacterlassociation/tagged/`, email (confirm permanent address — current slides use `info@vacterlsupportgroup.onmicrosoft.com`), safeguarding note, response-time expectation.
9. `/_meta` Footer only: charity number (TBC — applied Oct 2023 per article), privacy, cookies, accessibility statement, safeguarding.

## Navigation

Header: Home | What is VACTERL? | Support | Get-Together | Stories | Get Involved | Contact + prominent Donate button.
Footer: charity status, contact, socials, small-print links.

## URL / content rules

* Slugs lowercase with hyphens as above.
* Every content page: reviewed date, owner, `not medical advice` where relevant.
* Images require alt text + consent flag. No children's surnames without explicit consent.
* Videos: embedded player + full transcript on-page (accessibility + SEO).
