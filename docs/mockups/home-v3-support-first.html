Do you need support with VACTERL?

We are a friendly, parent- and adult-led support group in the UK. If your baby has just been diagnosed, or you live with VACTERL yourself, you are not alone.

Easy Read Summary:
VACTERL means a child is born with 3 or more body differences. Every child is unique. Children born with VACTERL grow up into adults, make friends, and live full lives. You can talk to us at any time.

What each letter means

  • V — Back bones (Vertebral anomalies in the spine)
  • A — Bottom opening (Anal anomalies / Anorectal malformation, repaired with surgery and bowel care)
  • C — Heart (Cardiac structural differences)
  • T and E — Windpipe and food pipe (Tracheo-oesophageal fistula / Oesophageal atresia)
  • R — Kidneys or arm bone (Renal or Radius differences)
  • L — Arms, hands and legs (Limb differences)

VACTERL happens in about 1 in 10,000 to 1 in 40,000 babies. It is not caused by anything a parent did. Note: This page is for support and is not medical advice. Always ask your hospital care team.

3 simple steps to get help

  1. Join our friendly community — Message us on our Facebook group or by email. Other parents and adults with VACTERL will welcome you.
  2. Come to the yearly Get-Together — Meet other families, children, and specialist nurses in person once a year.
  3. Use our partner charities — We can also connect you with TOFS (swallowing/TOF), Max's Trust (bottom/bowel care), VACTERL Visionaries (adult support), Family Fund (grants), and IPSEA (school help).

Worried about hospital appointments?

Hospital tests and blood draws can feel scary or overwhelming for children and adults. Our adult members recommend:

  • Bringing a Trauma Card to hand to nurses or doctors so they know what makes you anxious.
  • Taking a family member or friend as an advocate.
  • Using headphones, music, or puzzles to distract you during tests.
