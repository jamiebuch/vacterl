Do you need support with VACTERL?

We are a parent-led support group. We help children and families. We are friendly.

Easy Read: VACTERL means a child is born with 3 or more health differences. Each letter is a body part. We meet once a year. You can talk to us.
Get support now What does VACTERL mean? Join the group

What each letter means

  • V — back bones (vertebral)
  • A — bottom opening (anal)
  • C — heart (cardiac)
  • T and E — food pipe and windpipe link (tracheo-oesophageal fistula / atresia)
  • R — kidneys or arm bones (renal / radius)
  • L — arms and legs (limb)

About 1 in 10,000 to 40,000 babies. A doctor must confirm. This page is not medical advice. Ask your care team.

3 steps to get help

  1. Contact us — Facebook group or email. We reply when volunteers are free.
  2. Meet us — come to the yearly Get-Together or local pop-up.
  3. Stay in touch — join as a member. Help others if you can.
