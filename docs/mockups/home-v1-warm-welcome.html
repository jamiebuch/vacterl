What each letter in VACTERL stands for

VACTERL association is characterised by the sporadic, non-random association of specific birth differences. A person with three or more features in any combination may be recognised as fitting the association:

VVertebralBack bones / spine
AAnalBottom opening (ARM)
CCardiacHeart structure
TTracheo-Windpipe fistula (TOF)
EOesophageal*Food pipe atresia (OA)
RRenal / RadiusKidneys or forearm
LLimbArms, hands or legs

* TE — Fistula ± oesophageal atresia. VACTERL is not a single diagnosis with one cause. Prevalence is ~1 in 10,000 to 1 in 40,000 live-born infants (Solomon, 2011).

How we help children, adults & families

Advice & Peer Network

Practical peer advice and emotional support for children, young adults, and parents/carers from people with lived experience.

Lifelong Friendships

Opportunities for VACTERL children and young adults to meet each other, build self-esteem, and see positive adult role models.

Clinical & Trauma Awareness

Specialist talks (bowel/stoma care, ARM, continence) and practical self-advocacy tools like our appointment trauma card.

We collaborate & signpost
