## What is VACTERL? (from Bristol pop-up banner + slide 2)

> VACTERL association is characterised by the sporadic, non-random association of specific abnormalities.
> Each letter stands for a specific abnormality:
> * V — Vertebral anomalies
> * A — Anal anomalies
> * C — Cardiac anomalies
> * TE — Fistula +/- oesophageal atresia anomalies
> * R — Renal or Radius anomalies
> * L — Limb anomalies
>
> There is a wide range of manifestation. A person with three or more problems in any combination may be recognised as fitting in the VACTERL association. However, VACTERL is not a diagnosis. There is no one cause for the association of these problems in individuals.
>
> VACTERL/VATER association is typically defined by the presence of at least three of the following congenital malformations: vertebral defects, anal atresia, cardiac defects, tracheo-esophageal fistula, renal anomalies, and limb abnormalities.
>
> Prevalence is estimated at approximately 1 in 10,000 to 1 in 40,000 live-born infants.
>
> Why don't we know what causes it? "The differential diagnosis is relatively large … research methods offer promise that the causes of VACTERL association will be better defined in the relatively near future. Antenatal diagnosis can be challenging, as certain component features can be difficult to ascertain prior to birth." (Solomon, B.D, 2011)

## Aims of the support group (from banner)

* To develop a network that will provide advice and support to children and parents / carers of VACTERL children
* Provide opportunities for VACTERL children to meet each other and develop supporting friendships
* Generate opportunities for the VACTERL child
* Develop a system of promoting self esteem for the VACTERL child
* Advise the medical profession of the Association
